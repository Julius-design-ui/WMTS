About the Mountain Kingdom

Welcome to Lesotho, a country where mountains are more than scenery. They are part of the country's identity, history and everyday life.

Known as the Mountain Kingdom, Lesotho is a small kingdom surrounded by South Africa, yet its landscapes and culture give it a character entirely its own.

A Country Written in the Mountains

Lesotho's highlands stretch across dramatic mountains, valleys and plateaus. For generations, these landscapes have shaped the way communities travel, work and live.

Traditional Basotho culture remains an important part of life in the kingdom. The famous Basotho blanket, traditional clothing, mountain villages and the Basotho pony all form part of the country's unique identity.

This is a place where a journey can take you from modern towns to quiet mountain villages, often within the same day.

Lesotho at a Glance

  • Capital: Maseru
  • Location: Southern Africa
  • Known as: The Mountain Kingdom
  • People: Basotho
  • Language: Sesotho and English
  • Currency: Lesotho Loti (LSL)

Where Culture Lives

Lesotho's culture can be experienced through its music, clothing, food, traditions and communities. The Basotho blanket is one of the country's most recognisable cultural symbols.

The Basotho pony also has a special place in the country's story. Horses have long been important for travelling through the difficult terrain of the highlands.

Visitors can experience this culture not only in museums and landmarks, but also through the people and communities they encounter along the journey.

Why Visit Lesotho?

Lesotho offers something different from the ordinary tourist experience. Its mountains invite exploration, its villages tell stories, and its landscapes create moments that are difficult to forget.

  1. Experience breathtaking mountain landscapes.
  2. Discover Basotho culture and traditions.
  3. Explore historic places and landmarks.
  4. Experience outdoor adventures.
  5. See a different side of Southern Africa.
