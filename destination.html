Places Worth the Journey

Some places are visited. Others are remembered. Explore the landscapes, landmarks and hidden corners that make Lesotho a journey worth taking.

Maletsunyane Falls

Near Semonkong, the Maletsunyane Falls drops dramatically into a deep gorge, creating one of Lesotho's most striking natural landscapes.

The surrounding highlands make this more than a waterfall stop. It is a place to experience the scale and silence of the mountains.

Best for: Photography, hiking and adventure.

What to experience: Take in the dramatic waterfall, explore the surrounding highlands, and enjoy the mountain scenery.

Travel tip: Wear comfortable shoes and check local conditions before setting out on a hike.

Thaba Bosiu

Rising above the surrounding landscape, Thaba Bosiu is one of the most important historical sites in Lesotho. Its connection to King Moshoeshoe I makes this mountain more than a beautiful viewpoint.

It is a place where the history of the Basotho nation comes alive.

Best for: History, culture, and panoramic views.

What to experience: Explore the historic mountain associated with King Moshoeshoe I and enjoy panoramic views of the surrounding landscape.

Travel tip: Wear comfortable walking shoes and allow time to explore the historical site.

Ts'ehlanyane National Park

Step away from busy roads and into one of Lesotho's peaceful mountain environments. The park is surrounded by rugged highlands, native vegetation, and scenic walking trails, making it a natural escape for travelers who prefer slower adventures.

Best for: Nature, hiking, and peaceful escapes.

What to experience: Enjoy scenic walking trails, explore the native vegetation, and take in the rugged mountain landscape.

Travel tip: Wear comfortable walking shoes and bring warm clothing for changing mountain weather.

AfriSki Mountain Resort

When winter transforms the highlands, AfriSki becomes one of Lesotho's most distinctive adventure destinations. Snow, mountains, and outdoor activities create an experience that feels very different from the usual Southern African getaway.

Best for: Skiing, snow, and adventure.

What to experience: Enjoy snow activities, take in the dramatic highland scenery, and experience Lesotho's winter adventure atmosphere.

Travel tip: Check seasonal snow conditions before travelling and pack warm clothing.

Katse Dam

Discover one of Lesotho's most impressive engineering landmarks, set among the dramatic highlands. Katse Dam combines remarkable architecture with sweeping mountain scenery and peaceful views of the water.

Take in the impressive dam, admire the surrounding highland scenery, and enjoy the peaceful mountain atmosphere.

Best for: Scenic views, photography, and sightseeing.

What to experience: Take in the impressive dam, admire the surrounding highland scenery, and enjoy the peaceful mountain atmosphere.

Travel tip: Plan your journey ahead and check local access conditions before visiting.

Semonkong

Known for its beautiful mountain scenery, Semonkong offers a chance to experience the quieter side of Lesotho. Its rugged landscapes and traditional surroundings make it a memorable stop for visitors exploring the highlands.

Explore the mountain surroundings, enjoy the local scenery, and discover the landscapes around Semonkong.

Best for: Nature, photography, and cultural experiences.

What to experience: Explore the mountain surroundings, enjoy the local scenery, and discover the landscapes around Semonkong.

Travel tip: Allow enough travel time, as mountain roads can make journeys slower.
